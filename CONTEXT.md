# Voice Director

A run-aware director turns spoken or external inspiration into playable surprises.

## Language

**Card definition**:
An immutable design shared by cards with the same rules and artwork identity.

**Card instance**:
A particular card in a run. Its identity survives transformations and connects its persistent deck version to its combat copy.
_Avoid_: card ID when the definition or instance is ambiguous

**Wildcard**:
A reward card with one remaining transformation opportunity, usable after acquisition or on a later successful combat draw.

**Living Deck**:
The mode in which eligible ordinary deck cards can transform when drawn.

**Inspiration**:
Speech, chat or another external signal that influences a future card without itself causing a transformation.

**Candidate**:
A validated card definition ready for an eligible transformation opportunity.

**Lucky transformation**:
A deck-aware transformation seeking an unusually compelling twist, including dangerous directions with worthwhile payoffs.
